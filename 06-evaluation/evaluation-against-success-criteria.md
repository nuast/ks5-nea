# Evaluation Against Success Criteria

Evaluate outcomes directly against measurable success criteria using real evidence.

- Is each criterion met, partly met, or not met?
- What specific evidence supports that judgement?
- If unmet, what realistic improvement is needed?

> Weak practice warning: do not claim success without evidence links.
