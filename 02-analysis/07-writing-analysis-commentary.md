# Writing Analysis Commentary

Strong analysis is not just evidence collection. It is evidence plus decisions.

## Evidence-to-decision pattern

Use this structure repeatedly:

1. what you found
2. why it matters
3. what decision you made
4. where this appears in requirements/success criteria

## Sentence stems

- “Research with [stakeholder] showed that …”
- “This matters because …”
- “Therefore, requirement [ID] specifies …”
- “This will be measured by success criterion [SC ID] …”

## Mini example

> Survey responses showed that 72% of users need to find records by surname during lessons. This matters because speed is critical in lesson time. Therefore R4 requires surname search, and SC4 measures retrieval speed under 2 seconds.

## Avoid these weak patterns

- listing findings without decisions
- writing long transcript-style notes
- repeating the same point across multiple sections
