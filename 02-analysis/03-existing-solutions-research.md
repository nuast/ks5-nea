# Existing Solutions Research

## 1) Purpose

You are not showing off product names. You are showing what each product taught you.

## 2) What to look for

For each solution you review, analyse:

- key features
- strengths
- weaknesses
- usability ideas
- data handling approach
- what you will adapt (and what you will avoid)

Use: [Product Research Template](templates/product-research-template.md)

## 3) Good analysis pattern

For each product, write:

1. **What it does well**
2. **What does not fit your context**
3. **What you will carry into your own solution**
4. **Which stakeholder need this supports**

### Example

- Product has a calendar week view with conflict warnings.
- Strength: quick visual scheduling.
- Weakness: very complex permission model for school context.
- Decision: adopt week view and clash warning, keep permissions simple.
- Stakeholder link: teaching staff requested fast booking checks.

## 4) Common weak approach vs strong approach

**Weak:**

> I looked at three apps and they were good.

**Strong:**

> Product A showed an effective colour-coded weekly view, so I will use a similar visual layout. Product B had strong filters but too many admin steps; my version will keep one-click filtering for teacher and date only.
