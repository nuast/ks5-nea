# Stakeholders and Research Methods

## 1) Stakeholders in this project

A stakeholder is any person affected by the current problem or your proposed solution.

Common roles:

- **Primary user:** uses the system daily
- **Decision-maker:** approves features/priorities
- **Support/technical user:** helps maintain/administer the system
- **Occasional user:** uses a smaller subset of features

Use stakeholders who are:

- relevant to the problem
- available to give feedback
- knowledgeable enough to provide useful detail

Avoid weak choices like “random friend” if they do not use the system context.

Use: [Stakeholder Table Template](templates/stakeholder-table-template.md)

## 2) What to collect from stakeholders

Collect evidence that can become requirements:

- current frustrations
- tasks they do most often
- must-have features vs nice-to-have
- usability/accessibility needs
- constraints (time, devices, permissions)

## 3) Research methods that work

### Interviews / meetings

Best for depth and follow-up.

Good when:

- you need context and reasoning
- you need to clarify contradictions

Use: [Interview Question Bank](templates/interview-question-bank.md)

### Surveys / questionnaires

Best for wider input and basic counts.

Good when:

- many users need the same questions
- you want percentages/rankings

Use: [Survey Question Bank](templates/survey-question-bank.md)

### Existing product research

Use this alongside stakeholder research to compare feature ideas and usability approaches.

## 4) Qualitative vs quantitative findings (simple)

- **Qualitative:** comments, opinions, reasons
- **Quantitative:** counts, percentages, ratings

Example summary:

- 10/14 users said duplicate entries happen weekly. *(quantitative)*
- Users repeatedly asked for quick confirmation after saving. *(qualitative)*

## 5) How to present evidence properly

Do not paste huge raw transcripts.

For each research activity, summarise:

1. method used
2. who took part
3. key findings
4. impact on project decisions

Writing frame:

> Interview with [role] found that [finding]. This led to [decision/requirement], because [reason].
