# Analysis Completion Checklist

Use this before moving to Design.

## Problem and approach

- [ ] I have a clear problem statement (users, issue, impact, boundary).
- [ ] I explained why this specific problem suits a computational solution.
- [ ] I avoided generic claims and used context-specific reasoning.

## Stakeholders and research

- [ ] I identified suitable stakeholders and explained why they were chosen.
- [ ] I used at least two research methods (for example interview + survey).
- [ ] I summarised findings (not transcript dumps).
- [ ] I included both qualitative and quantitative evidence where possible.

## Existing solutions

- [ ] I reviewed similar products/systems.
- [ ] I explained what I learned from each one.
- [ ] I clearly stated what I will adapt and why.

## Requirements and success criteria

- [ ] Requirements are numbered and testable.
- [ ] Each requirement is justified with stakeholder/research evidence.
- [ ] Success criteria are measurable and linked to requirement IDs.
- [ ] Each success criterion has a clear test method.

## Scope and practicality

- [ ] I stated realistic limitations and out-of-scope items.
- [ ] I justified limitations professionally.
- [ ] I listed relevant hardware/software requirements only where needed.

## Quality and readiness

- [ ] My commentary links findings to decisions.
- [ ] There is no major duplication across analysis files.
- [ ] Analysis content sets up Design, Testing, and Evaluation clearly.
