## Evidence flow (analysis to evaluation)

1. Identify needs and define numbered requirements in Analysis.
2. Convert key needs into measurable success criteria.
3. Design each major part with requirement links.
4. Build iteratively and record evidence per iteration.
5. Test iteratively and as a full system, including stakeholder/usability testing.
6. Evaluate directly against criteria and requirement links using recorded evidence.
