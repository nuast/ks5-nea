# Milestone Log

Use this as a lightweight progress/iteration log. Record meaningful updates, decisions, and next actions so your development journey is evidenced clearly.

- What was completed?
- What changed from plan?
- Why was that change made?
- What is next?

> Weak practice warning: avoid entries like "did coding" with no detail or evidence reference.
