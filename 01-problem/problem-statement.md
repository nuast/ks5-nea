# Problem Statement

Define the real-world problem, who experiences it, and the scope boundaries of your project. This should set up strong analysis and requirements.

- What is the current problem?
- Who has this problem and how often?
- Why does this problem matter?
- What will your project include?
- What is deliberately out of scope?

> OCR reminder: avoid vague statements; make the problem concrete and contextual.
